Methodology — How Alumni Advantage is Measured

1. Career Outcomes (30%)

  • Median earnings 10yr post-entry (College Scorecard)
  • Employment 6mo (IPEDS + surveys)
  • Grad school enrollment
  • Fortune 500 / startup founders per 1k alumni (LinkedIn enrichment)

2. Alumni Support (20%)

  • Alumni giving rate (IPEDS, CASE)
  • Endowment per FTE (NACUBO, IPEDS)
  • Alumni network size (alumni association)
  • Career services staff per student

3. Academic Quality (15%)

  • 6yr graduation rate (IPEDS)
  • First-year retention
  • Student-faculty ratio
  • Research spend per student (NSF HERD)

4. Financial Health (15%)

  • Endowment 5yr growth (NACUBO, 990)
  • Tuition reliance = tuition / total revenue (990, audited)
  • State appropriation stability (publics, IPEDS Finance)
  • Moody's / S&P credit rating

5. Value & ROI (20%)

  • Net price low-income ($0-30k) (Scorecard)
  • Pell graduation gap
  • Loan default rate (Scorecard)
  • Debt at graduation, 10yr ROI = (earnings - HS earnings) - net price

6. Public Filings (trust signal, unweighted)

  • IPEDS — mandatory federal, all Title IV
  • IRS 990 — private nonprofits, Schedule D endowment
  • Audited financials — GAAP, endowment footnotes
  • College Scorecard — earnings, debt, default, net price
  • NSF HERD — research expenditures
  • State audit — publics, single audit

Scoring normalizes each metric 0-100 within Carnegie class, then weighted sum. Public filings boost confidence interval, not score.
