Endowments fund the present and the future.

• You create perpetual funding for your philanthropic priorities
• Your endowment gift is invested:
    • A portion of investment return provides annual funding for scholarships, professorships, or any
      program you choose
    • The rest of investment return compounds to grow your endowment

• Your endowment relieves fiscal pressures – current and future:
    • Pressures: Rising education costs balanced against flat or modest tuition increases, student debt
      capacity, and competition for limited state resources
    • Relief: Endowments create a new permanent source of funding

How much can the University spend from your endowment?

• Spending distributions are made from the endowment account to
  the spending account on a uniform and regular basis.
• To smooth the highs and lows of an endowment’s market value,
  spending distributions are based on a formula.
• The formula for spending distributions is 4.5% of the pool’s 13
  quarter average market value per unit.
   • The 13 quarter average market value per unit will vary year to year
     and historically is about 85-95% of the current market value per unit,
     depending on market performance.

• Distributions are calculated at the end of December for use the
  following July, which is the start of UVM’s academic year.
