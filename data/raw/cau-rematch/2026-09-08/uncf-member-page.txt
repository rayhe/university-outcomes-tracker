Clark Atlanta University is a private, coeducational, four-year university in the historic heart of Atlanta, GA.

It is the only university in the historic Atlanta University Center, a consortium of 8,000 primarily African-American scholars. It is designated by the Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching as a "Doctoral University - High Research Activity. ... Clark Atlanta University has produced more than 29,000 alumni in myriad fields of professional, civic and social endeavor.
