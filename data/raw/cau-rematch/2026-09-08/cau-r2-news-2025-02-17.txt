# Clark Atlanta University Renewed as Prestigious R2 Research Institution in 2025 Carnegie Classifications

As one of only two private HBCUs with the R2 designation, CAU celebrates its exceptional academic distinction and pioneering contributions to scholarly research

Clark Atlanta University (CAU) is thrilled to announce that it is one of only two private HBCUs to achieve the *Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production* designation in the 2025 Carnegie Classifications.

Maintaining the R2 designation signifies that Clark Atlanta University meets rigorous standards in research activity, including an average annual expenditure of at least $5 million on research and the awarding of 20 or more research doctorates each year. The university's recent research expenditures have reached $10,320,000 for fiscal year 2023, and it has averaged 38 research doctorates over the past three academic years (2020-21, 2021-22, and 2022-23).
